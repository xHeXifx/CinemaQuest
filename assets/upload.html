Cinema videos

Maps on your Quest and their Cinema videos. Download a map's video on this device (the YouTube link, or the yt-dlp command), then upload it here. It's saved into the map's folder with the name Cinema expects.

Maps without a config can get one too: use Add video to pick a YouTube video and set its offset. Changes apply from the next time you start the song. Keep Beat Saber open in the menu while using this page.
